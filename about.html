About Zivah Shipping Logistics

A global leader in supply chain operations, freight handling, and door-to-door delivery since 2012.

📁 Company Profile

Who we are!

Zivah Shipping and Logistics is a dynamic shipping and logistics company committed to delivering reliable, efficient, and cost-effective transportation solutions across domestic and international markets.

With a strong global network and experienced logistics professionals, we specialize in freight forwarding, customs clearance, warehousing, and supply chain management. Our mission is to simplify complex logistics processes while ensuring timely delivery, cargo safety, and complete customer satisfaction.

Mission

To provide reliable, efficient, and cost-effective shipping and logistics solutions that connect businesses to global markets. We are committed to delivering cargo safely and on time while maintaining the highest standards of professionalism, integrity, and customer satisfaction.

We strive to simplify complex supply chains through innovation, technology, and dedicated service excellence.

Vision

We aspire to establish ourselves as the premier global logistics partner, universally recognized for setting the benchmark in operational excellence and innovative supply chain solutions. Our commitment extends beyond mere transportation, as we dedicate ourselves to implementing sustainable practices that protect our environment while driving efficiency across all operations. By fostering trust and collaboration, we aim to build enduring long-term customer relationships that grow alongside our business expansion.

Our Services

Our services cover Ocean Freight (FCL & LCL), Air Freight, Express Courier, Rail Freight, Road Transportation, Intermodal Shipping, Vessel & Aircraft Chartering, Break Bulk, and Project Cargo Handling.

We also provide specialized equipment solutions including Reefer, Open Top, and Flat Rack containers to accommodate temperature-sensitive and oversized shipments.
