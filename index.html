Our Services

We Offer the following services

Ocean Freight

We provide reliable and cost-effective ocean freight solutions for global import and export shipments. Our Full Container Load (FCL) service is ideal for large consignments, while Less than Container Load (LCL) offers economical options for smaller cargo.

Air Freight

Our air freight service is designed for urgent, high-value, and time-sensitive shipments. With strong airline partnerships, we offer fast transit times and flexible scheduling options. We ensure safe handling of both general and dangerous goods cargo with full compliance and tracking support.

Road Freight

Our company offers comprehensive road freight services including Full Truck Load (FTL) and Less than Truck Load (LTL). Our network ensures timely and safe delivery across domestic and international routes. GPS-enabled tracking provides transparency and shipment visibility.

Intermodal / Multimodal Shipping

Our multimodal solutions combine sea, air, rail, and road transport for optimized routing and cost efficiency. We manage the entire logistics chain under one contract, ensuring seamless cargo transfers. This service reduces transit time while maintaining high operational control.

Vessel Chartering

For oversized or bulk shipments, we provide vessel and aircraft chartering services tailored to your specific requirements. Our charter solutions offer flexible scheduling and dedicated transport capacity. This service is ideal for large-scale industrial and commercial cargo movements.

Project Cargo Handling

We specialise in handling heavy-lift, oversized, and complex project cargo. Our team conducts detailed route planning, risk assessment, and cargo supervision from origin to destination. We ensure safe, efficient, and professionally managed execution of large logistics projects.

Break Bulk Services

Our break bulk services are designed for cargo that cannot be containerized. We handle heavy machinery, industrial equipment, steel products, and oversized goods with expert care. Direct vessel loading ensures safe and efficient transportation.

Rail Freight

Rail freight offers a cost-effective and environmentally friendly solution for inland and cross-border shipments. We provide secure containerized transport with reliable scheduling. This service is ideal for bulk cargo and long-distance land transportation.

Express / Courier Services

Our company offer dependable domestic and international courier solutions for the documents and small parcels. Our express services guarantee fast and secure deliveries with proper documentation support. Ideal for urgent business shipments requiring speed and efficiency.

Special Equipment Solutions

We provide specialized containers including Reefer (temperature-controlled), Open Top, and Flat Rack units. These solutions are ideal for perishable goods, oversized cargo, and heavy equipment. Our specialized equipment ensures safe handling of unique shipment requirements.

Customs Clearance

Our professional customs brokerage team ensures smooth and timely clearance of import and export shipments. We handle documentation, duties, regulatory compliance, and DG cargo approvals. Our expertise minimizes delays and ensures seamless cargo movement.

Door-to-Door Delivery

We offer complete door-to-door logistics solutions from pickup to final delivery. Our service includes transportation, customs clearance, and last-mile delivery. Clients benefit from a single point of contact and end-to-end shipment management.
