Primary Logistics Hubs

Our strategically positioned operations centers handle bulk load distribution, customs brokerage, and cargo transfers.

Domestic (Pakistan)

Gateway Ports: Karachi Port (KICT), Port Qasim, Gwadar Port

Our domestic operations manage bulk distribution, customs clearance, and door-to-door freight forwarding across Pakistan. Features a dedicated regional transport fleet linking Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Multan, Faisalabad, and Peshawar.

Gateway Ports: Dubai, Singapore, Shanghai, Rotterdam, Newark

Our international network links Pakistan to the global supply chain with weekly ocean freight sailings (FCL and LCL) and expedited air cargo connections. We coordinate directly with overseas customs agencies and bonded warehouse facilities.
