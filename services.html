World-Class Logistics Services

Comprehensive freight forwarding and container cargo solutions tailored to your operational requirements.

What We Do

Delivering dependability across land, air, and sea. Explore our complete logistical offerings.

🚢

Ocean Freight

We provide reliable and cost-effective ocean freight solutions for global import and export shipments. Our Full Container Load (FCL) service is ideal for large consignments, while Less than Container Load (LCL) offers economical options for smaller cargo.

✈️

Air Freight

Our air freight service is designed for urgent, high-value, and time-sensitive shipments. With strong airline partnerships, we offer fast transit times and flexible scheduling options. We ensure safe handling of both general and dangerous goods cargo.

🚛

Road Freight

Our company offers comprehensive road freight services including Full Truck Load (FTL) and Less than Truck Load (LTL). Our network ensures timely and safe delivery across domestic routes. GPS-enabled tracking provides transparency and visibility.

🔄

Intermodal / Multimodal

Our multimodal solutions combine sea, air, rail, and road transport for optimized routing and cost efficiency. We manage the entire logistics chain under one contract, ensuring seamless cargo transfers and reduced transit times.

🛳️

Vessel Chartering

For oversized or bulk shipments, we provide vessel and aircraft chartering services tailored to your specific requirements. Our charter solutions offer flexible scheduling and dedicated transport capacity for large-scale cargo movements.

🏗️

Project Cargo Handling

We specialise in handling heavy-lift, oversized, and complex project cargo. Our team conducts detailed route planning, risk assessment, and cargo supervision from origin to destination to ensure professional and safe execution.

📦

Break Bulk Services

Our break bulk services are designed for cargo that cannot be containerized. We handle heavy machinery, industrial equipment, steel products, and oversized goods with expert care. Direct vessel loading ensures safe transportation.

🚊

Rail Freight

Rail freight offers a cost-effective and environmentally friendly solution for inland and cross-border shipments. We provide secure containerized transport with reliable scheduling, ideal for bulk cargo and long-distance land transit.

✉️

Express / Courier Services

Our company offers dependable domestic and international courier solutions for documents and small parcels. Our express services guarantee fast and secure deliveries with proper documentation support and speed.

❄️

Special Equipment

We provide specialized containers including Reefer (temperature-controlled), Open Top, and Flat Rack units. These solutions are ideal for perishable goods, oversized cargo, and heavy equipment ensuring safe handling.

🔑

Door-to-Door Delivery

We offer complete door-to-door logistics solutions from pickup to final delivery. Our service includes transportation, customs clearance, and last-mile delivery, leaving clients with a single point of contact.

📝

Customs Clearance

Our professional customs brokerage team ensures smooth and timely clearance of import and export shipments. We handle documentation, duties, regulatory compliance, and DG cargo approvals, minimizing terminal delays.
